Performance baseline (phase0-baseline plus the perf instrumentation). Made by app/tools/perf/bench.sh, twice, one after the
other on the same build; run 1 in full, then the numbers that matter from run 2. Machine: Apple M5, macOS 27.0.1.

How to read it: "freeze" is a gap over 50 ms between 16 ms heartbeats on the app's main thread (a timer) or in the page (a
setInterval, "page heartbeat", n = how many). "nbFlush" is the page's whole state made for the app (closing, switching);
"persist" is the app writing it into the library's files. The first "switch: start" line (about 3.5 s) is the time since the
app started, not a cost. "2 open a paper" is PDFKit loading the paper: its freeze moves between about 60 and 170 ms from run
to run, so judge a change there by repeats. "0 start in another project" and "start" are the test's own set-up (the app
settling at launch), not part of the numbers below.

Library arguments (app/tools/perf/mklib.py <dir> <pdf> <note paragraphs> <essays> <essay paragraphs> <points>, then options):
  small   60 3 30 40                                           notes 48 KB, 125 KB of notebook state
  big     1500 40 80 400                                       notes 920 KB, 40 essays, 3.0 MB of state
  images  300 6 40 100 --images 6 --image-px 200 --equations 30   42 PNG pictures (5.0 MB raw, 6.7 MB as base64 inside the
                                                               documents), 30 block + 30 inline equations per document; 7.0 MB

                          small    big      images      (run 1 / run 2)
typing in the notes        0 freezes over 50 ms in the app and in the page, in every run, on all three libraries
                           except the images library's run 1: one freeze of 51 ms in the app (run 2: none)
nbFlush                    3 / 4 ms   11 / 26 ms   46 / 34 ms
persist (the big save)     4.2 / 8.3 ms for 125 KB   29.6 / 40.4 ms for 3004 KB   53.3 / 43.1 ms for 6970 KB
open a paper, worst freeze 76 / 80 ms   79 / 78 ms   63 / 166 ms

Earlier results on a library of the big kind (an older build, another session): 0 freezes over 50 ms typing, persist 46 ms for
3 MB, nbFlush 25 ms, worst freeze opening a paper 148 ms. These agree with what is above to within the run-to-run spread.
